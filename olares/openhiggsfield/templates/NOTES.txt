1. After this chart is merged to main, wait for GitHub Actions to publish
   ghcr.io/wooj-commits/open-higgsfield:0.1.0 and confirm the GHCR package is
   Public (anonymous pull; no cluster imagePullSecret).
2. On the Olares node:
     olares-cli chart lint ./olares/openhiggsfield
     olares-cli chart package ./olares/openhiggsfield
     olares-cli market upload openhiggsfield-0.1.0.tgz
     olares-cli market install -s upload --watch
3. At install, enter AUTH_SECRET, AUTH_USERNAME, AUTH_PASSWORD,
   HF_API_BASE_URL, and HF_API_KEY. Olares injects them as .Values.olaresEnv.*
   into Secret openhiggsfield, then into the container via envFrom.
4. Open the Desktop entrance while signed in as the Olares account. The
   entrance is private. Then sign in to the studio itself.
